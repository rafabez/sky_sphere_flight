Flight Training Course

Welcome, pilot! Your mission is to fly through the sequence of rings as quickly as possible.

  • Objective: Fly through all the rings in order.
  • Scoring: Earn 100 points for each ring you successfully navigate.
  • Guidance:
    • The GREEN ring is your next target.
    • The YELLOW arrow points towards the next target ring.
    • BLUE rings are ones you've already passed through.
    • GREY rings are further along the course.
  • Controls (Keyboard):
    • W / S: Forward / Backward
    • A / D: Strafe Left / Right
    • R / F: Move Up / Down
    • Q / E: Roll Left / Right
    • Arrow Up / Arrow Down: Pitch Up / Down
    • Arrow Left / Arrow Right: Yaw Left / Right
    • Shift: Move Slower (Hold)
  • Controls (Mouse):
    • Drag to look around (Pitch/Yaw).
  • Controls (Touch):
    • One Finger Drag: Look around (Pitch/Yaw).
    • Two Finger Pinch: Zoom In / Out (Adjust Field of View).

Click the close button (top right), press Escape, or click the button below to start.
